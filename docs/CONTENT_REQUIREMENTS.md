# Electra Solar — Content Guidelines & Editorial Requirements

## 1. Editorial Voice & Positioning Principles

Electra Solar’s editorial voice must reflect that of a **senior power electronics engineering firm** combined with a customer-centric energy partner:
- **Authoritative & Technically Rigorous:** Use exact engineering terminology (e.g., *Bi-directional DSP Controlled Inverter*, *Zero-break static transfer < 5ms*, *100% Phase Unbalance Capability*, *Galvanic Isolation*).
- **Practical & Solution-Oriented:** Frame technical parameters in terms of commercial value (e.g., eliminating diesel generator costs, protecting sensitive CNC machines from grid voltage spikes).
- **Honest & Verifiable:** Never exaggerate solar yields, promise 100% free electricity without qualification, or claim imaginary patent numbers. Always state realistic operating parameters.
- **Indian Market Context:** Emphasize resilience against frequent grid brownouts, uncoordinated single-phase tripping, monsoon solar variability, and high commercial peak tariffs.

## 2. Content Standards by Section

### 2.1 Product Catalogues & Specification Pages
Every product listing must provide:
1. **Model Classification:** Clear indication of phase (Single Phase vs. Three Phase) and topology (Transformer-based vs. High-Frequency Transformerless).
2. **Clear Power Ratings:** Continuous kVA / kW capacity, along with transient overload handling (e.g. 150% for 60 seconds).
3. **Solar MPPT Voltage Bounds:** Exact DC minimum and maximum operating voltages to allow solar installers to verify PV string calculations.
4. **Battery Bus Voltage:** Precise nominal DC voltage requirements (48V, 96V, 240V, 360V).
5. **Real Safety Protections:** Full breakdown of built-in hardware protections (Overvoltage, Short Circuit, Reverse Polarity, Lightning Arrestor SPD, Islanding Protection).

### 2.2 Turnkey Industry Solutions
Each solution page must follow this structured flow:
1. **The Core Industrial Problem:** Grid unreliability, costly DG run hours, or sensitive equipment reboots.
2. **The Electra Engineered Solution:** Synergy between solar PV, EnerTech bi-directional hybrid inverters, and LFP battery storage.
3. **Verified Benefits:** Tangible reductions in diesel expenditure, ROI timeframe (typically 3–4 years), and power quality stability.
4. **Recommended Hardware Models:** Direct links to the appropriate inverter and battery SKUs.

### 2.3 Installation Projects & Case Studies
All case studies must adhere to verifiable facts:
- **Client Classification:** Industry segment (e.g. Automotive Engineering, Cold Storage, Super-speciality Hospital).
- **Location:** Geographic district in Maharashtra (e.g. Chakan MIDC, Hinjawadi Phase 2, Baramati).
- **System Architecture:** Exact equipment installed (Inverter kVA + Solar kWp + Battery kWh).
- **Operational Impact:** Measurable metrics such as estimated annual kWh generation or diesel reduction percentage.

### 2.4 EnerTech Partnership Attribution
Every reference to EnerTech must strictly follow the approved wording:
> *"Electra Solar is the official distributor and turnkey engineering partner of EnerTech UPS Pvt. Ltd. (Pune), delivering industry-leading industrial solar hybrid inverters, off-grid PCUs, and energy storage systems across Maharashtra and India."*
